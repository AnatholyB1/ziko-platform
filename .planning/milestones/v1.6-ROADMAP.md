## Overview

Five phases deliver the mobile athlete side of the coach-athlete link: a mandatory "Mon coach" plugin with a 3-state invitation UX (no code → preview → linked), revocation from settings, full fr+en i18n, and optional AI tools — all against zero new backend endpoints (Phase 25 routes reused). A parallel design catch-up thread (Phase 30) was dropped as out of scope for this mobile-only workstream.

Design-first was enforced: Phase 28 (Mon coach Figma) preceded Phase 29 (implementation).
