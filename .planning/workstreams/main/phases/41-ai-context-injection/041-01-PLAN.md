| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-41-01 | Tampering | dashboard_context in /chat/stream body | accept | dashboard_context is coach-provided context injected into their own session's system prompt; no cross-coach leakage; values are stringified metrics (no executable content) |
