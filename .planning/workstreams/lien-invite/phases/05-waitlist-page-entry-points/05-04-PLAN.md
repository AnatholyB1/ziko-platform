## Resolved at plan time — the `/coachs` button labels change with their destination

D-01 changes where the two `/coachs` buttons lead. It does not, on its face, say anything about what
they say. But both currently read "Rejoindre la bêta privée" / "Join the private beta", and after
this change neither of them joins anything — they open a waitlist. A button whose label promises
immediate access and whose destination is a queue is a misrepresentation, and this milestone's
`REQUIREMENTS.md` rules out exactly that class of claim, twice, in its Out of Scope table.

So the labels and their supporting notes are rewritten alongside the targets, within the copy
constraints already set: the offer stated as fact, the real cap, no deadline, no urgency device. This
is not a new section on `/coachs` and does not reopen D-06 — the page's structure is unchanged and
the change is confined to four strings and two hrefs.
