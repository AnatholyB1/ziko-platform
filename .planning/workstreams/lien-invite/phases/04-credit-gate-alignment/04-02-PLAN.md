## Plan-time correction to 04-RESEARCH.md and 04-PATTERNS.md

Both documents prescribe modelling `grant_premium_credits()` on `deduct_ai_credits`
(`026_ai_credits.sql:89-134`) and both reproduce its `ON CONFLICT (user_id, source,
idempotency_key) DO NOTHING` clause with no `WHERE` predicate. That clause cannot infer
`idx_credit_tx_idempotency`, which is a **partial** unique index — PostgreSQL raises "no unique or
exclusion constraint matching the ON CONFLICT specification" and the function throws.

This is not a hypothesis. `supabase/migrations/028_fix_earn_rpc_and_quota_tracking.sql` exists
because exactly this bug shipped in `earn_ai_credits`, and its header comment names the symptom
verbatim. Its corrected function is also structurally better than the deduct analog for a grant: it
inserts the ledger row first, reads `ROW_COUNT`, returns early on a duplicate, and only then ensures
the credits row exists and increments the balance. That ordering is what makes a re-run a true
no-op — the skeleton in 04-PATTERNS.md increments the balance *before* the conflicting insert, which
double-funds on every retry while leaving only one ledger row behind.

`028` is therefore the analog for this plan, not `026`. The research and pattern documents are
superseded on this point.
